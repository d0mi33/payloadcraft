An agent is software that reads, decides and acts on its own. That makes it useful, and it makes it a new kind of insider. This guide starts from zero: what zero trust means, why agents need it, the five ways agents get attacked, and the order to build defenses in. Read the parts in order; each one builds on the last. It's a restructured walk through Anthropic's eight-chapter [Zero Trust for AI Agents](https://claude.com/resources/guides/zero-trust-for-ai-agents/building-for-the-next-threat-landscape) guide.
